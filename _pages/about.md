I am a theoretical physicist, currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Paris Interdisciplinary Energy Research Institute (LIED/PIERI, UMR 8236). I completed my PhD in theoretical and computational physics at CEA Paris-Saclay (IRAMIS/SPEC, SPHYNX group), under the supervision of Sébastien Aumaître and Hervé Bercegol.

I am currently working on complex networks through generative modelling: growing networks from simple rules and comparing them with large empirical data sets, to explain what shapes real networks and to model the dynamical processes that run on them.

My path to networks is somewhat unusual. I trained in theoretical high energy physics, at École Polytechnique and École Normale Supérieure (ICFP Master's). My first research project was on the causal set approach to quantum gravity, where spacetime is described as a network of partially ordered elements. That work remains an active interest, and it led me to bring the same viewpoint to the questions I find most urgent, those raised by the ecological transition. In both cases, the guiding question is how macroscopic properties emerge from the growth of a discrete network.
