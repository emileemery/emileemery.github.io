I am a statistical physicist of complex networks. I reconstruct networks from large, heterogeneous and incomplete data sets, explain the resulting structures with parsimonious generative rules, and model the dynamical processes that run on them. The systems I have studied so far are infrastructural, ecological and social.

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Paris Interdisciplinary Energy Research Institute (LIED/PIERI, UMR 8236), Université Paris Cité / CNRS, where I apply this approach to the energy transition: how electricity grids grow, how much material they contain, and what it takes to build and renew them worldwide.
