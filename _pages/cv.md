Research profile
======
Statistical physicist of complex networks. I reconstruct complex networks from large, heterogeneous and incomplete data sets, explain the resulting structures with parsimonious generative rules, and model the dynamical processes that run on them. The systems studied so far are infrastructural, ecological and social.

Skills
======
* **Statistical physics**: network analysis; generative models; scaling laws derived analytically; comparison against null models; universality across systems
* **Network reconstruction**: large networks assembled and validated from incomplete and inconsistent sources; missing layers inferred rather than assumed
* **Dynamical processes**: dynamics on networks modelled and simulated; evolution and stability derived analytically, including under time-varying couplings; processes optimised over a fixed topology
* **Ecological interactions**: time-varying interaction networks inferred from ecological time series; rewiring quantified and its effect on coexistence assessed
* **Metabolism**: energy and material resource balances written on graphs; stocks and renewal flows derived from network structure and projected under scenarios
* **Machine learning**: neural networks built and trained in `tensorflow`; MNIST digit classification implemented from scratch in `numpy`; regression, clustering, model selection and cross-validation
* **Programming**: Python, LaTeX, Mathematica, Bash, Git, CAML, SQL
  * Complex networks and hypergraphs: `networkx`, `xgi`
  * Large-scale data processing and visualisation: `pandas`, `geopandas`
  * Machine learning and regression: `tensorflow`, `scikit-learn`
* **Software**: Gephi, VS Code, GitHub, Zotero, Inkscape, Logseq, Activity Browser
* **Reproducibility and open science**: reproducible analysis workflows, code and data released with the publications; training in Data Management Plans and open-science fundamentals
* **Peer review**: IOP Publishing Peer Review Excellence training
* **Languages**: French (native); English (fluent: publications, conferences and teaching); Italian and Spanish (working knowledge); Lojban (beginner)
